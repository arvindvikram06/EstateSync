# EstateSync Knowledge Base

Each section below (marked with ##) becomes one retrievable chunk. Content is written in plain, user-facing language describing platform flows and navigation. No credentials, no internal technical details.

## What is EstateSync

EstateSync is a rental property platform that connects property owners and tenants. Owners list properties, manage applications, create leases, and receive rent payments. Tenants search for properties, request visits, apply for rentals, sign leases, and pay rent. The platform handles the entire rental journey from listing to lease to payment in one place. To start renting or listing, create an account and complete identity verification first.

## Creating an Account

Go to the Sign Up page and enter your name, email address, and a password. After signing up, a verification email is sent to your inbox. Open the email and click the verification link to activate your account. Until you verify your email, you cannot log in. Once verified, log in to access your dashboard. To resend the verification email, use the Resend Verification option on the login page.

## Logging In and Logging Out

Go to the login page and enter your email and password to log in. Your email must be verified before you can log in. After logging in you are taken to your dashboard. To log out, click your avatar in the top right corner of the navigation bar and select Log Out.

## Resetting a Forgotten Password

Click Forgot Password on the login page and enter your registered email address. A password reset link is sent to your email. Open the link and enter your new password. The reset link expires after one hour, so use it promptly. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder or request a new link.

## Managing Your Profile

Click your avatar in the navigation bar and select Profile to view your account details. Update your name and phone number from the profile page and save the changes. To change your password while logged in, open Profile and click Change Password, then enter your current password followed by your new password.

## Understanding Verified Badges

Your profile shows your verification status. A KYC verified badge means your identity has been approved and you can apply for rentals. An owner verified status means you have upgraded to an owner account and connected your payment account, so you can list properties and receive payments. These badges confirm you have completed the required steps to rent or list on the platform.

## What KYC Is and Why It Is Required

KYC is identity verification. Complete KYC before applying for a rental property or becoming a property owner. It is a one-time process where you submit identity documents for review. Once approved, your account is marked as verified and you can proceed with all rental activities on the platform.

## Starting Your KYC

Go to your dashboard and open the KYC section. Click Get Started to create a draft and fill in your basic personal details. Your draft is saved, so you can leave and return to continue before submitting.

## Uploading KYC Documents and Submitting

Upload four documents on the KYC page — Aadhaar card front, Aadhaar card back, PAN card, and address proof. Place each document in its designated slot and make sure it is clear and fully visible. Once all documents are uploaded, click Submit to send your application for review. A notification is sent once a decision is made.

## KYC Review and Outcome

After submitting, your KYC enters a review state and a team member checks your documents. When approved, your account is marked verified and you receive a notification. When rejected, you receive a notification with a reason explaining what to correct. Fix the issue and resubmit your documents. There is no limit on how many times you can resubmit.

## Tenant Dashboard Overview

Your Tenant dashboard is where you manage your rental activity. It has sections for your rental applications, your leases, your saved properties, your bills and payments, and your complaints. Use the sidebar to move between sections. Each section shows the current status of your items so you always know where things stand.

## Searching and Browsing Properties

Go to the Properties page from the main navigation bar to see all available properties as cards. Each card shows the property photo, location, number of bedrooms, and monthly rent. Click any card to open the full property detail page with photos, description, amenities, rent, and deposit details. Only approved and active properties appear on this page.

## Filtering Properties

Use the filter bar on the Properties page to narrow your search. Filter by city or state using the search bar, by property type, by number of bedrooms, and by rent range using a minimum and maximum amount. The bedroom filter shows properties with that number of bedrooms or more. Filters work together and results update automatically as you apply them.

## Saving a Property

Click the Save button on a property card or detail page to save a property for later. View all saved properties from the Saved Properties section on your dashboard. Click Unsave to remove one. Saving does not notify the owner and does not reserve the property.

## Requesting a Property Visit

Open the property detail page and click Request a Visit to ask the owner for a viewing. The request is sent to the property owner, who approves or rejects it. You receive a notification once the owner responds. After approval, coordinate the visit timing with the owner.

## Applying for a Rental Property

Open the property listing and click Apply. A verified KYC is required to apply. Once submitted, your application is sent to the owner for review, and you receive a notification when the owner decides. Track all your applications from the Applications section on your dashboard, where each shows its status as Pending, Approved, or Rejected.

## Withdrawing an Application

Open the application from the Applications section on your dashboard and click Withdraw, as long as it has not yet been approved. Once withdrawn, the application is cancelled and does not move to the lease stage.

## Application Statuses Explained

Your rental application moves through several statuses. Pending means it has been submitted and is waiting for the owner to review it. Approved means the owner accepted it and a lease can be created. Rejected means the owner did not accept it. Withdrawn means you cancelled it yourself before a decision. Completed means the application ran its full course — a lease was created from it and that lease has since ended, either by expiring or by you moving out. You can see the status of each application in the Applications section of your dashboard.

## Becoming a Property Owner

To list properties, upgrade your account to Owner. Your KYC must be approved first. Open your Profile from the navigation bar and click Become an Owner. Your account is upgraded and you gain access to the Owner dashboard with property listing and management features.

## Connecting Your Payment Account as an Owner

After becoming an owner, connect a payment account to receive rent from tenants. From your Owner dashboard, click Connect Payment Account and follow the secure bank account linking steps. Once connected and verified, you can list properties and receive payments. This step is required before your listings can go live.

## Owner Dashboard Overview

Your Owner dashboard gives a full view of your rental business. It has sections for your properties, rental applications, leases, payouts, and complaints against your properties. The overview shows a summary of active properties, pending applications, and upcoming payments. Use the sidebar to move between sections and manage everything in one place.

## Property Statuses Explained

Your properties move through several statuses. Draft means the listing is still being prepared and not yet submitted. Pending Approval means it has been submitted and is awaiting review. Active means it is approved and visible to tenants. Leased means a tenant has an active lease on it. Available Soon means the current lease is ending within three months. Rejected means the listing was not approved and needs corrections. You can see each property's status on the Properties section of your dashboard.

## Property Lifecycle from Listing to Re-Listing

A property moves through a full lifecycle. It starts as a Draft while you prepare the listing, then becomes Pending Approval once you submit it. After approval it becomes Active and appears to tenants. When a tenant's lease becomes active, the property turns to Leased and is no longer shown to new tenants. When that lease has less than three months left with no renewal, it is marked Available Soon so it can start attracting new tenants while the current lease finishes. Once the lease expires or the tenant moves out, the property returns to Active and is open for new applications again. If a renewal is approved before the lease ends, the property goes back from Available Soon to Leased.

## Listing a New Property

Go to your Owner dashboard and click Add Property to open the step-by-step listing wizard. Complete each step in order — steps cannot be skipped. Progress is saved as a draft at every step, so you can leave and return to continue. Once all steps are done, the listing is ready to submit.

## Filling in Property Details

In the first wizard step, enter the property title, type, address, city, state, number of bedrooms, monthly rent, deposit amount, and a description. The rent and deposit amounts are used later in the lease, so enter them accurately. Click Next once all required fields are filled.

## Uploading Property Photos

In the images step, upload clear photos showing different rooms and areas of the property. Good photos make the listing more attractive and improve your chances of applications. Remove any photo by clicking its delete icon. At least one photo is recommended before submitting.

## Adding Property Amenities

In the amenities step, select all amenities available at the property, such as parking, water supply, and power backup. Tenants use amenities to filter and compare properties. Amenities can be updated later from the property detail page on your dashboard.

## Uploading Property Ownership Documents

Upload ownership documents to verify you are the legal owner of the property. These documents are reviewed privately and are never shown to tenants. Upload the required documents, such as the property deed or ownership certificate, in the documents step. They are kept securely and accessed only during approval.

## Submitting a Property for Approval

Once all wizard steps are complete, click Submit for Approval. After the listing is approved, it becomes visible to tenants on the public properties page. If rejected, you receive a notification with the reason. Make the corrections and resubmit for approval.

## Editing a Property

Open the Properties section of your Owner dashboard and select the property you want to change. You can update its details, photos, and amenities. Save your changes when done. Draft properties can be edited freely before submission, and active listings can also be updated.

## Reviewing Rental Applications as an Owner

When a tenant applies for your property, you receive a notification. Open the Applications section on your Owner dashboard and open the application to view the tenant's details. Choose Approve or Reject. Only one application can be approved per property. The tenant is notified of your decision either way.

## Creating a Lease

After approving an application, open it on your Owner dashboard and click Create Lease. Fill in the lease terms — start date, end date, rent amount, deposit amount, payment frequency, and notice period. Once created, the tenant is notified that a lease is ready and waiting for signatures. A lease agreement document is prepared automatically from the terms you entered.

## Choosing a Payment Frequency

When creating a lease, choose how often rent is billed. The options are Monthly, Quarterly, Half Yearly, and Yearly. This decides how often rent bills are generated for the tenant during the lease. Pick the frequency that matches your agreement with the tenant.

## Updating Lease Terms Before Signing

The owner can update the lease terms while the lease is still waiting for signatures and before anyone has signed. Open the lease from your dashboard and edit the terms. The lease agreement document is regenerated with the updated details. Once either party has signed, the terms can no longer be changed.

## Signing a Lease

After a lease is created, both the owner and tenant sign it. Open the lease from your dashboard and click Sign, then enter your full name and provide your signature. The tenant signs first, followed by the owner. When only one party has signed, the lease shows as Partially Signed. After both parties sign, the lease goes for a final review before becoming active.

## What Happens After Both Parties Sign

Once both the owner and tenant have signed, the lease is sent for a final review and you receive a notification that it is under review. After the review, the lease is either approved or rejected. When approved, the lease becomes active and two bills are generated — a security deposit bill and the first rent bill. When rejected, both parties are notified with a reason and the lease is cancelled.

## Understanding Lease Statuses

A lease moves through several statuses. Pending Approval means it has been created and is waiting for signatures. Partially Signed means one party has signed and the other has not. Pending Admin Approval means both parties have signed and it is in final review. Active means it is approved and in effect. Expired means the lease term has ended. Terminated means it was ended early. Voided means it was cancelled before becoming active. You can see the current status on the lease page.

## Downloading the Lease Agreement

You can download the lease agreement document from the lease page. Before signing, a draft agreement is available to review. After both parties have signed, a fully signed copy that includes both signatures is available to download. Open the lease from your dashboard and use the download option to save a copy for your records.

## Voiding or Terminating a Lease

Before both parties have signed, the owner can cancel the lease by opening it and clicking Void. After a lease becomes active, the owner can end it early by clicking Terminate on the lease page. Both actions notify the tenant and cannot be undone.

## Requesting a Lease Renewal as a Tenant

Before your lease expires, open the lease page on your dashboard and click Request Renewal. The owner is notified and decides whether to approve or reject the request. You receive a notification with the outcome. Request a renewal early so the property is not marked for new tenants as the lease nears its end.

## Approving or Rejecting a Renewal as an Owner

When a tenant requests a lease renewal, you receive a notification. Open the lease on your Owner dashboard and review the renewal request. To approve, set a new end date for the lease, which must be later than the current end date. Choose to approve with the new date or reject the request. The tenant is notified of your decision either way.

## What Happens After a Renewal Is Approved

When the owner approves a renewal, the same lease continues with its end date extended to the new date the owner set. A new lease is not created and there is nothing to sign again — your existing lease simply runs longer. The tenant receives a notification confirming the new end date. If the property had been marked Available Soon because the lease was near its end, it returns to Leased status. Rent bills continue as normal under the extended term.

## Submitting a Vacate Notice

To move out, open the lease page and submit a Vacate Notice. Enter your intended exit date and submit it. The owner reviews the notice and accepts or rejects it. The notice period stated in your lease must be respected when choosing your exit date, otherwise a penalty may apply.

## Handling a Vacate Notice as an Owner

When a tenant submits a vacate notice, you receive a notification. Open the lease and review the notice, including the tenant's intended exit date. Choose Accept or Reject. On acceptance, the lease is scheduled to close on the exit date, and any applicable penalty is added to the tenant's bills. On rejection, the tenant is notified and the lease continues as normal.

## Property Marked Available Soon Before Lease Ends

When a lease has less than three months remaining and no renewal has been requested, the property is automatically marked as Available Soon and you receive a notification. Submit a renewal request before the lease nears its end date to avoid this. If the lease expires without renewal, the property becomes available for new tenants.

## Moving Out Before Your Vacate Notice Date

When you submit a vacate notice with a specific exit date and move out earlier, the lease remains active until the agreed exit date. Rent bills continue until the notice period ends, and the lease closes only after the exit date passes. Set the exit date on your vacate notice to match the date you actually plan to move out.

## Penalty for Vacating Before the Notice Period

Every lease has a minimum notice period that must be respected. When the exit date on your vacate notice is earlier than the notice period allows, a penalty applies. The penalty amount is based on your security deposit and your lease terms, and it is shown before you confirm. When the owner accepts the notice, a penalty bill is added to your account due on the exit date, and your security deposit may be partially or fully forfeited depending on the lease terms. Give enough advance notice as stated in your lease to avoid a penalty.

## Viewing Your Rent Bills

After a lease becomes active, rent bills are generated automatically based on the payment frequency. Open the lease page and go to the Bills section to see all bills. Each bill shows the amount, due date, and status — Pending, Paid, or Overdue. Bills stay listed here whether they are paid or still due.

## Paying a Rent Bill

Open the lease page, go to the Bills section, and click Pay on the bill you want to settle. You are taken to a secure payment page where you complete the payment with your card. After the payment succeeds, the bill status updates to Paid automatically and you receive a confirmation notification. Your paid bills remain visible in the Bills section for your records.

## Viewing Your Payment History

Open the lease page and go to the Payments section to see a record of all payments you have made on that lease. Each entry shows the amount paid and the date. This gives you a complete history of your rent and deposit payments for the lease.

## Security Deposit Bill

When your lease is approved and becomes active, a security deposit bill is generated automatically. The deposit amount is set by the owner in the lease terms. It appears in the Bills section of your lease and is paid the same way as rent — click Pay and complete the payment. The deposit is held as a guarantee and may cover unpaid dues or damages at the end of the lease.

## Late Payment Penalty on Overdue Bills

When a rent bill is not paid by its due date, a late payment penalty may be added automatically, and you receive a notification. The penalty appears in the Bills section alongside the overdue rent. Pay rent bills before the due date shown on each bill to avoid penalties.

## Platform Fee

EstateSync charges a small platform fee on each rent payment. The fee is deducted automatically when the tenant pays, and the remaining amount is transferred to the owner's connected payment account. The fee is handled in the background with no action needed from either party.

## Requesting a Standard Payout as an Owner

Open the Payouts section of your Owner dashboard and click Request Payout to withdraw your available balance. Standard payouts take one to two business days to reach your bank account. Earnings are not transferred automatically — request a payout to withdraw them.

## Requesting an Instant Payout as an Owner

Open the Payouts section of your Owner dashboard and choose Instant Payout to receive your earnings sooner. Instant payouts arrive much faster than standard payouts and are available when you have funds ready for instant withdrawal.

## Raising a Complaint

Open your Tenant dashboard and click Raise a Complaint. You need an active lease on the property to raise a complaint about it. Select the type of issue, set the priority, and describe the problem clearly. Attach photos to support the complaint if needed. After you submit, the relevant parties are notified.

## Complaint Types and Priorities

When raising a complaint, choose the type that best fits the issue — Maintenance, Billing, Noise, Safety, Cleanliness, or Other. Set a priority to show how urgent it is — Low, Medium, High, or Critical. Choosing the right type and priority helps the owner understand and address your issue faster.

## Tracking Complaint Status

Track complaints from your dashboard. A complaint starts as Open, moves to In Progress when it is being addressed, and then to Resolved. As a tenant, mark a resolved complaint as Closed once you are satisfied. You receive a notification whenever the complaint status changes.

## Handling Complaints as an Owner

Complaints raised by tenants on your properties appear in the Complaints section of your Owner dashboard. Open a complaint to see its type, priority, description, and any attached photos. Update its status as you work on it — move it from Open to In Progress and then to Resolved. The tenant is notified of each status change.

## Notifications

EstateSync sends notifications for important events — when your KYC is reviewed, when a lease is created or signed, when a rent bill is generated, when a payment is received, and when a complaint is updated. Click the bell icon in the top navigation bar to view all notifications. Mark them as read one by one or all at once.

## Why You Cannot Apply for a Property

To apply for a rental property, your identity verification must be approved first. If the Apply button is not working or you are blocked from applying, check that your KYC is complete and approved on your dashboard. If your KYC is still pending or was rejected, you cannot apply until it is approved. Complete or resubmit your KYC, and once it is approved you will be able to apply.

## Why You Cannot List a Property

To list a property, two things must be done first. Your account must be upgraded to an owner account, which requires an approved KYC. You must also connect your payment account so you can receive rent. If you cannot add a property, check your Profile to confirm you have become an owner and check your Owner dashboard to confirm your payment account is connected. Once both are done, you can list properties.

## Why You Cannot Create a Lease

To create a lease, the tenant's rental application must be approved first, and the tenant must have an approved KYC. Open the approved application on your Owner dashboard to start creating the lease. If the option is unavailable, confirm that you have approved the application and that the tenant is verified.

## Why You Cannot Edit a Lease

Lease terms can only be changed while the lease is waiting for signatures and before either party has signed. Once the tenant or owner has signed, the terms are locked and cannot be edited. If you need to change a signed lease, the owner can void it before it becomes active and create a new one.

## Did Not Receive the Verification or Reset Email

If a verification or password reset email did not arrive, first check your spam or junk folder. For verification, use the Resend Verification option on the login page to get a new email. For password reset, request a new link from the Forgot Password page. Reset links expire after one hour, so always use the most recent one.

## What Happens If a Payment Fails

If a rent or deposit payment does not go through, the bill stays unpaid and you can try again. Open the lease, go to the Bills section, and click Pay on the same bill to retry the payment. Make sure your card details are correct. Once a payment succeeds, the bill updates to Paid automatically.

## What Happens If My KYC Is Rejected

If your KYC is rejected, you receive a notification with the reason. Open the KYC section on your dashboard to see what needs to be corrected. Common reasons are unclear or incomplete documents. Fix the issue, re-upload the correct documents, and submit again. There is no limit on how many times you can resubmit.

## Is My Data and Documents Safe

EstateSync keeps your personal documents secure. Identity documents and ownership documents are stored privately and are only accessed during the verification and approval process. Sensitive documents are never shown publicly or to other users. Property photos you upload are public because they appear on your listing, but private documents remain protected.

## Do I Have to Pay to Use EstateSync

Browsing properties, applying for rentals, and managing your account are free for tenants. As a tenant you only pay your rent, deposit, and any penalties through the platform. Owners pay a small platform fee that is automatically deducted from each rent payment. There is no separate charge to sign up or list properties beyond this fee.

## How Long Does Review Take

KYC verification, property approval, and lease approval are each reviewed by the team before moving forward. You receive a notification as soon as a decision is made on any of them. While an item is under review, its status shows as pending on your dashboard so you can always see where it stands.

## Can I Get My Security Deposit Back

Your security deposit is held as a guarantee during the lease. At the end of the lease it may be used to cover any unpaid dues, penalties, or damages, based on your lease terms. If you end the lease before the notice period, part or all of the deposit may be forfeited. Any remaining deposit is handled according to the terms agreed in your lease.
