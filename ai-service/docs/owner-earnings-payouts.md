# How owner earnings arise

Owner earnings are associated with successful tenant payments for the owner’s leases and the platform’s settlement rules. A tenant beginning checkout does not create available owner funds. The payment must succeed and the relevant processing must complete. The Owner dashboard’s Earnings area is the source for the displayed balance and payout availability.

# Payment-account prerequisite

The owner’s connected payment account must remain ready to receive and withdraw funds. Incomplete verification, required provider information, or an unsupported account state can prevent payouts even when rental activity exists. Resume secure payment-account onboarding from the Owner dashboard and follow the provider’s instructions. Do not send banking credentials or verification codes through chat.

# Available and unavailable balances

The amount shown as available for payout can differ from gross tenant payments because of platform fees, processing state, refunds or adjustments supported by the system, and funds that have not yet become withdrawable. The assistant should not infer a balance from rent amounts. Owners must use the authenticated Earnings view for actual figures.

# Standard payout

Choose Request Payout from the owner earnings area and select the supported standard option when funds are available. Standard payouts follow the delivery estimate shown by the payment process; business days and external banking processing can affect arrival. Submitting a payout request does not guarantee instant arrival.

# Instant payout

Instant payout is available only when the connected account, destination, region, and available balance satisfy the provider’s eligibility rules shown by the application. If the option is absent, use a standard payout or complete any required account step. The assistant should not promise eligibility or a precise arrival time that the application does not display.

# Repeated requests and status checks

After requesting a payout, check its status before submitting another request. A processing payout should not be treated as missing simply because it has not reached the bank. If a payout fails, follow the displayed correction path and verify the connected destination through the secure provider flow.

# Currency display

Different stages can display currency according to the tenant charge, platform records, and payout provider behavior. Owners should rely on the labels and amounts shown for each stage rather than assuming two differently presented values are identical. The assistant can explain the distinction but should not calculate exchange rates or guarantee the final bank conversion.

# Privacy and support

Only the authorized owner and staff views can access owner settlement records. Tenants do not receive an owner’s bank or payout details. When contacting support, share the EstateSync payout reference and status, not full account numbers, passwords, one-time codes, or screenshots exposing private financial details.
