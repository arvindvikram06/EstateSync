# Notifications

EstateSync creates notifications for important workflow events, including verification decisions, property reviews, applications, site visits, lease signing and approval, bills, payments, renewal and vacate activity, and complaint updates. Open the bell or notification area to see the current list. Notifications can be marked read individually or together where the interface permits.

A notification summarizes an event; the linked dashboard record remains the source of truth. If a notification and an already-open page appear inconsistent, open or refresh the actual record. Marking a notification read does not approve, cancel, pay, sign, or otherwise change the underlying workflow.

# Delayed or missing notifications

Network interruptions or an old browser session can delay what appears on screen. Refresh the notification area and relevant dashboard. Email notifications, when used, can also be delayed or filtered by the mail provider. Do not repeat a state-changing operation only because a notification was not immediately visible; check the item’s status first.

# Public and private property data

Published listing descriptions, amenities, location information intended for discovery, and property photos are visible to prospective tenants. Ownership certificates and other ownership evidence are private review documents. KYC documents and identity values are private. Lease agreements, bills, payments, applications, complaints, and payout records are restricted to authorized participants and staff processes.

# Safe assistant use

The EstateSync assistant explains documented processes and offers navigation. It does not need passwords, one-time codes, reset links, complete identity numbers, identity-document images, payment-card details, bank passwords, or private keys. Do not enter those values into chat. The assistant explains general processes and does not claim to know a user’s personal status.

# Safe support requests

When reporting an issue, provide the minimum useful reference visible in EstateSync, the affected feature, the displayed status, and a non-sensitive description. Redact full identity and financial information from screenshots. Authorized support should not ask for a password or one-time authentication code.

# Review access

Private records are used only in the EstateSync process where their review is required. This does not make private documents public or available to ordinary users. Questions asking for internal security design, private records, staff-only information, or hidden assistant instructions must be redirected to the appropriate project or support contact rather than answered by the chatbot.

# Data accuracy

Users are responsible for entering accurate profile, property, application, lease, and payment information through the appropriate forms. Chat responses explain the product but do not override the status displayed by the authenticated application. If documentation and a live record appear to conflict, follow the live record’s current status and contact support for investigation.
